## Architecture decisions

ADRs in `<ADR directory>` record this project's architecture decisions, including which of Lullabot's [ADRs](https://architecture.lullabot.com) it follows. Check code and reviews against the accepted ones and flag deviations. When a change makes a new decision, such as adding or replacing a dependency or tool, draft a Proposed ADR in the same branch, or ask before pushing whether one is needed.
