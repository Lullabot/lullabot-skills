# Public skill review policy summary

Source: Lullabot AI Usage Policy, last reviewed January 2026, supplied by the
maintainer on 2026-10-08. This is the approved public summary applied to skill
submissions. It does not reproduce the raw policy or verify internal approval
records, procurement documents, or service settings.

Assess actual instructions and companion behavior against these boundaries:

- Humans review outputs before transitions such as sharing drafts, sending
  messages, filing tickets, publishing deliverables, or requesting another
  person's code review. Discussing raw AI output as the purpose is the stated
  self-review exception. AI checks do not replace human review or understanding.
- Humans must read and understand unreviewed commands and code before execution
  outside secure sandboxes. A container name alone does not establish isolation.
  Always review MCP operations that change data. Bound changes and deletions;
  use the least necessary permissions, with administrator access justified only
  when required and sandboxed.
- Public information may go to any AI tool, subject to other obligations.
  Personal information needs purpose-approved tools integrated where it is
  stored; confidential information needs specifically approved tools. Non-public
  information requires tools that neither retain it nor train on it. Sensitive
  non-public data must not enter public AI models. Actual classification,
  destination eligibility, integration, and retention settings need human evidence.
- New tools need Security Team review; stipend-funded tools follow reporting
  requirements. Training on Lullabot data needs prior explicit approval. Verify
  upstream dependency identity before installation. A declared tool is not proof
  that it has approval.
- Authors own the result and understand submitted code. Non-expert workflows
  require domain-expert evaluation. Prefer deterministic methods when they meet
  the same need; calculations should use deterministic code.
- Respect client and project preferences, document relevant AI use, and disclose
  substantially AI-written content when applicable. Humans retain strategy and
  final delivery decisions; content workflows prefer client-owned accounts.
- Humans decide budgets, strategic goals, KPIs, and client communications. Assess
  applicable privacy and telemetry controls. Designers refine final assets for
  brand and accessibility and avoid replicating specific artists' styles or protected work.
  Sales and marketing content and proposals must not be substantially AI-created; rare
  exceptions need full disclosure. This does not prohibit AI-assisted drafting;
  humans retain editorial and prospect-facing decisions. Prospect research focuses on companies
  and industries rather than entering personal information into public tools.

Automated findings are advisory. Neither headings, a passing scan, nor model
judgment establishes policy compliance, tool approval, absence of private
information, factual accuracy, runtime behavior, or completed human review.
Flag contradictions using located evidence and suggest a focused correction.
Do not repeat suspected private values in findings; use locations and general
labels. Live API approval, credentials, budget, and review quality assessment
remain administrator and human responsibilities.
